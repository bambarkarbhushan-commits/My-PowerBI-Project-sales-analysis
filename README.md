# 🛍️ Myntra Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

The Myntra Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance and uncover business insights from Myntra sales data.

The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor key metrics such as Total Sales, Total Orders, Average Sales, regional performance, product performance, and monthly sales trends through intuitive visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Track overall sales performance
- Monitor order volume and average sales
- Analyze sales trends over time
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare sales across categories
- Understand customer segment contribution
- Enable data-driven decision making

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards
- 💰 Total Sales
- 📦 Total Orders
- 📈 Average Sales

### Visualizations

#### 1. Monthly Sales Trend
- Line Chart
- Displays sales performance by month
- Helps identify seasonal trends and growth patterns

#### 2. Category-wise Sales Analysis
- Bar Chart
- Compares sales across product categories
- Highlights best-performing categories

#### 3. Segment-wise Sales Distribution
- Donut Chart
- Shows contribution of different customer segments
- Useful for customer analysis

#### 4. Top Products Table
- Product Name
- Total Sales
- Identifies high-performing products

### Interactive Filters
- Region Filter
- Order Date Filter
